Algorithm engine

Mathematical models behind the verdict.

JaccardSet overlap

Measures shared unique terms against total unique terms to detect lexical similarity.

CosineVector angle

Compares word frequencies in vector space to reveal structural similarity beyond exact copy.

DiceBinary overlap

Emphasizes common matching terms while penalizing differences in dominated vocabularies.

LevenshteinEdit distance

Captures small textual edits and word substitutions that indicate near-copy transformations.

LCSSequence match

Measures the longest shared sequence to detect recurring phrase structures within documents.

Rabin-KarpRolling hash

Uses a rolling hash to scan repeated windows and detect pattern overlap efficiently across texts.
